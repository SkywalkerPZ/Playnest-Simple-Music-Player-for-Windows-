# Changelog

What's new in each version of Playnest. The newest version goes at the
top. When you release, the section for that version is what people see in
the update window, so write it for them: short, plain lines starting
with "- ".

## 0.2.3 - 2026-10-10

## 0.2.0 - 2026-09-27

### A whole new look
- Meet the classic player: the player, equalizer and playlist stacked in one neat, compact window.
- Press LIB to open your library beside it: search, playlists, liked songs, downloads and more.
- Four skins to choose from in Settings → Appearance: Y2K chrome (the new default), Classic green, Black & green and Midnight blue.
- Prefer the old full-window look? Switch the layout to Modern in the same place.
- The equalizer now has a preamp slider and a presets menu.
- The playlist window has ADD, REM, SEL and MISC buttons to add, remove, shuffle, sort or save your list as a playlist.

### Easier to install, and it keeps itself working
- Works on any Windows PC: on first launch Playnest downloads what it needs to play music by itself. Nothing else to install.
- The music engine updates itself every day, so search and playback keep working when YouTube changes things.
- Playnest now updates itself too. You'll see what's new, like this, and can update with one click.

### Smoother
- Uses much less CPU while playing, so it's lighter on your PC and battery.

## 0.1.0 - 2026-09-13

### The first version of Playnest
- Search for any song or artist and play it straight away, with a "Songs only" filter to hide long mixes.
- Playlists: create, rename and reorder them, or import a playlist from YouTube.
- Like songs with the heart, and download any song to listen offline.
- Queue, play next, shuffle, repeat, crossfade between songs, and a sleep timer.
- Suggestions and autoplay based on what you listen to.
- Artist pages with your most-played tracks and more from them.
- Lyrics that follow along with the song.
- Listening stats: your top artists and songs, and how much you've listened.
- Equalizer with presets, and volume levelling so every song plays at a similar loudness.
- Keyboard shortcuts, global hotkeys and a tray icon for controlling music from anywhere.
- Back up your library to a file and restore it on another PC.
